# exxyshop.com — Site Audit (Oct 2026)

## Platform
- **Shopify**, default free **Dawn** theme, almost no customisation.
- No image logo: the header shows the word "EXXY" in the theme's default font.
- Pages: Home, About Us, Products (/collections), Projects, Contact Us, Cart, Log in.
- Homepage: one hero photo (Gigi Burger Bar) + "Shop now" button. Nothing else.
- Contact: basic form (name, email, phone, comment). No phone number, WhatsApp, address or social links visible.

## Business model (what the site tells us)
- Egyptian outdoor-furniture manufacturer; metal industry since 1999.
- About page stresses **residential and commercial projects**.
- Projects page shows **hospitality work**: Gigi / EDNC, Maine, Canelé, Mulliri, Mochi Do — cafés and restaurants (one terrace faces the Giza pyramids).
- **Every product is priced LE 0.00** → online checkout is not really used. Real sales almost certainly happen through inquiry / quotation.
- Likely key audience: **restaurant, café and hotel owners, and interior designers**, plus villa owners.

- Descriptions are minimal (mostly empty for chairs).

## Photography
- Mostly white-background studio shots, 1–5 per product, varied sizes and styles.
- ⚠️ Many photos appear to be **catalogue photography from European design brands**, not photos of EXXY's own production. To ask EXXY: do they produce these designs, and do they have photos of their own pieces? This affects both the pitch and what we model in 3D.
